DroidBridge LAN 0.4.1 test fix

Cause fixed:
0.4.0 loaded libnetty_quiche.so from the Fabric/Knot classloader before DroidBridge LibPatcher's e4mc bridge ran. LibPatcher then tried to load the same native from its system-classloader context, Android rejected the duplicate classloader ownership, and the bridge fell back to the obsolete adapted/glibc native path.

0.4.1 behavior:
- DroidBridge LAN stages and SHA-256 verifies the Android libnetty_quiche.so.
- It sets link.e4mc.native_path.
- It DOES NOT call System.load itself.
- DroidBridge LibPatcher owns the actual native load exactly once when stock e4mc initializes Quiche.
- Official e4mc stays external and unmodified.
